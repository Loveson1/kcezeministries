About the author

Kelechukwu Chukwuebuka Eze

Kelechukwu Chukwuebuka Eze is the president of KC Eze Ministries, with a vision to raise young people as ministers of the gospel as they excel professionally. He is a missionary to young people with over 18 years of experience and a transformational speaker with a deep passion for teenagers, young adults, and professionals.

He also leads The Shift Africa Youths Initiative, hosts Impact Arena, and serves as Team Lead at Youth Missions Global. He is married to Favour Eze, and they are blessed with a son, Blessed.
